Solara Bay is an original open-world crime game set in a sun-soaked coastal city of the same name. You play Jax Marlowe, a driver fresh back in town and broke, working off a debt for Mara Quinn, who runs a garage in Old Town. Explore the city on foot or behind the wheel, steal cars, outrun the Solara Bay PD as your wanted level climbs, run six story missions — getaways, a bank job, a stolen sports car — plus taxi fares and street cash, with combat, a live minimap, police roadblocks, and a save that keeps your progress.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. WASD to move, mouse to aim, click to shoot, E to enter and steal cars, Shift to sprint, R to reload, P to pause, M to mute.
